# Tournament workspace

- Opening a tournament shows a persistent top-level tab bar: Overview,
  Categories, Registrations and Cash desk.
- Selecting a tab keeps the same tournament selected and adds that tab to the
  Back/Forward screen history. Opening a category from Categories or
  Registrations keeps the return route in history.
- Categories contains the active category list and category creation form.
- Registrations lists active categories and opens the selected category's
  workspace. Each category has Registrations, Draw, Matches and Results tabs.
  Entries, attendance and registration status are managed in its Registrations tab.
- Cash desk shows the existing player-oriented tournament cash desk.
- Draw, Matches and Results remain visible within each category and explain
  that their competition workflows are still in development.
- Tournament navigation labels are available in Serbian and English and remain
  keyboard accessible at narrow window widths.
